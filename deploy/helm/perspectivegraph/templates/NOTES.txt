⚠  SECURITY: auth.apiTokens is empty - the API and ingestion are UNAUTHENTICATED.
   PerspectiveGraph is a map of how to attack the org: anyone who can reach the
   Service/Ingress can read every attack path and write suppressions/tickets.
   Before exposing this beyond a trusted cluster, set a token, e.g.
     --set auth.apiTokens="$(openssl rand -hex 16):admin"
   and sign ingestion with --set ingest.hmacSecret=... - or let the chart generate
   both with --set secrets.generate=true.
